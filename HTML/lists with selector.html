How to prepare boiled rice

Rice eating cooking procedures
  1. Ingredients needed
    1. Two cups of rice
    2. A pot
    3. Salt
    4. 225 cl of water
  2. Wash the rice 20 times until it is clean
  3. Boil the water that has been taken above
  4. After the water is boiled,pour the rice
  5. Leave the rice for about 3 minutes to cook
  6. After the rice is soft,add salt to your taste
  7. Serve with vegetables
  8. Enjoy!
Note:Salt can be added to your taste
